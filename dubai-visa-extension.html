1. Visa Overview & Highlights

If you are currently in Dubai or the UAE on a 30-day or 60-day tourist visa and wish to extend your holiday, stay with family longer, or explore business opportunities, our Inside-Country Visa Extension allows you to renew your legal stay without the expense and inconvenience of flying out or doing border runs.

2. Who is this Visa Ideal For?

Tourists Extending Vacations

Stay longer in Dubai to enjoy more attractions, shopping festivals, and leisure events.

Visiting Relatives & Parents

Extend family visits comfortably without requiring elderly parents to travel back and forth.

Gain additional time while finalizing employment contracts, trade licenses, or residency visas.

Avoiding Overstay Fines

Prevent costly daily immigration fines by extending your visa before your grace period expires.

3. Required Documents & Specifications

Original Passport Color Scan (Bio Page) Must have at least 6 months remaining validity from your scheduled travel arrival date. Clear color scan with all text readable.
Recent Passport-Sized Photograph Clear color photo with a white background, sharp focus, 80% face coverage, and no reflections or sunglasses.
Confirmed Flight & Accommodation Details Return flight ticket or onward connection proof, plus hotel booking or host residence address in Dubai.
National ID / GCC Iqama (if applicable) For GCC residents or specific nationalities, submit valid local residency proof or national identity card.
