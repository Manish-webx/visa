The Dubai 30 Days Tourist Visa is the most popular UAE e-Visa for vacations and family visits. It allows a 30-day stay from arrival, gives 60 days to enter after approval, and is extendable inside the UAE without exiting.

1. Visa Overview & Highlights

The Dubai 30 Days Tourist Visa is the most sought-after travel document for international tourists visiting Dubai, Abu Dhabi, and the other emirates. It allows you to stay up to 30 continuous days from the date of your first entry, giving you ample time to discover world-class landmarks like Burj Khalifa, Palm Jumeirah, desert safaris, and luxury shopping destinations.

2. Who is this Visa Ideal For?

Leisure Tourists & Sightseers

Perfect for tourists wanting a comprehensive holiday across Dubai, Abu Dhabi, and Sharjah.

Family & Friends Visits

Ideal for visiting family members, relatives, or friends residing in the UAE.

Business & Exhibitions

Suitable for attending corporate summits, trade expos like GITEX, and industry conferences.

First-Time UAE Travelers

The easiest and most straightforward visa option for first-time visitors with high approval rates.

3. Required Documents & Specifications

Color Passport Scan (Front & Back) Passport must remain valid for at least 6 months from your Dubai entry date with clear bio details and Machine Readable Zone (MRZ).
Passport-Size Photograph (White Background) Sharp, unedited recent portrait photo on a solid white backdrop; no glasses glare, hats, or cropped shoulders.
Return Flight Ticket & Stay Details Confirmed return itinerary plus hotel booking or UAE sponsor/relative Emirates ID copy.
Minors Under 18 (If Traveling) English-translated birth certificate, parents' passport copies, and NOC if traveling with one parent or guardian.
