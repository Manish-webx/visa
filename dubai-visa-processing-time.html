Dubai Visa Processing Time: What You Need to Know

Author: Dubai Visa Assistance Team • Published: Jan 5, 2026 • Last Updated: Jan 12, 2026
  1. Home
  2. Blog
  3. Processing Time Guide
Immigration Advisory: Visa processing times depend on immigration security screening and system workload. Timelines cannot be legally guaranteed by any third-party agency.

One of the most frequent questions from travelers is: "How long will it take for my Dubai visa to be approved?" Understanding standard processing schedules and potential delay factors helps you plan your flight and hotel bookings with peace of mind.

1. Standard Processing Timeline (24 – 72 Hours)

For common 30-day and 60-day tourist visas, the typical electronic processing window is 24 to 72 business hours after clean, verified documents have been submitted to immigration authorities.

2. UAE Immigration Working Days

UAE federal government departments operate on a Monday through Friday working week (with half-day Friday). While automated electronic systems process applications continuously, manual security checks or supervisor approvals occur during official business hours (GST).

3. Factors That May Extend Processing Time

  • Name Similarity (Security Screening): If an applicant shares a common name with an individual on an international alert list, manual identity clearance takes additional time.
  • Previous Overstay Record: Any prior unresolved overstay fines or uncancelled residency files in the UAE must be manually investigated.
  • Unclear Scans: If the passport MRZ or photo requires re-upload, processing will reset from the re-submission date.
  • Public Holidays: Eid al-Fitr, National Day, and New Year periods experience heavier immigration backlogs.

When Should You Apply?

Because single-entry visas give you a 60-day window to enter the UAE from date of issuance, the safest approach is to submit your visa request 10 to 14 days before your planned flight. This ensures ample buffer time without risking visa expiry before your departure.
