Why Is My Dubai Visa Taking Too Much Time?

Reviewed by: Dubai Express Visas Compliance Desk • Published: Nov 28, 2026
  1. Home
  2. Blog
  3. Why Dubai Visa Takes Too Long
Immigration Advisory: Visa processing speed and manual security clearances are governed solely by GDRFA Dubai and UAE ICP. Timelines cannot be overridden by any private agency.
Quick Answer: Why Your Dubai Visa Is Delayed

A standard Dubai tourist e-Visa normally takes 24 to 72 business hours. If your visa is taking longer than 3–4 working days, the most common causes are: (1) a manual security / name-similarity check, (2) UAE weekend or public holiday backlogs (Saturday–Sunday & half-day Friday), (3) an uncancelled previous UAE visa still open in immigration records, (4) blurry passport MRZ / photo scans awaiting re-upload, or (5) late filing by a sub-agent. Do not submit a duplicate application while one is pending.

Waiting on an approved e-Visa when your flight date is approaching is stressful. Understanding why UAE immigration systems sometimes hold an application—and what actions actually help—keeps you from making costly mistakes like filing duplicate applications.

1. Normal Turnaround vs. Actual Delay

Before assuming your application is stuck, verify how many UAE immigration working hours have elapsed since actual lodgment on GDRFA or ICP (not just since you paid an agent):

Application Tier Expected Window When It Is Considered Delayed
Express Fast-Track (14/30/60d) 4 to 12 hours More than 24 business hours without issuance
Regular Tourist Visa (30/60d) 24 to 72 business hours More than 4 UAE working days (excluding Sat–Sun)
UAE 5-Year Multi-Entry Visa 3 to 7 business days More than 10 working days (bank/insurance review)

2. Top 8 Reasons Your Dubai Visa Takes Too Much Time

1) Name Similarity & Manual Security Screening

This is the #1 reason an otherwise clean tourist visa gets held beyond 72 hours. If your first and last name match or closely resemble an individual on an international or regional alert database, automated approval pauses and routes the file to an immigration security officer for manual verification. Providing full parent names and clear national ID details helps clear false matches faster.

2) Uncancelled Previous UAE Visa or Open Residency File

If you previously visited or worked in the UAE and your old tourist visa has not expired—or an old employment/residence permit was never formally cancelled—GDRFA/ICP will hold or stall the new entry permit until the prior file is closed.

3) UAE Weekend & Public Holiday Backlogs

UAE federal government offices operate Monday to Friday (with half-day Friday). If you apply Thursday evening or Friday afternoon, manual officer reviews effectively resume Monday morning GST. Major holidays such as Eid al-Fitr, Eid al-Adha, UAE National Day (Dec 2), and New Year create multi-day processing queues.

4) Blurred Passport MRZ or Non-Compliant Photo

When Optical Character Recognition (OCR) cannot read the two-line Machine Readable Zone (MRZ) at the bottom of your passport bio page—or detects camera flash glare, cropped edges, or a patterned photo background—immigration flags the file as "Returned for Modification", resetting the clock until corrected files are uploaded.

5) Unresolved Overstay Fines or Past Travel History

Any unpaid daily overstay penalties (see our Dubai Overstay Fine Rules) or an unresolved absconding report from a prior trip automatically places new applications into manual compliance review.

6) Additional Nationality or Profile Verification

Depending on passport nationality, age bracket, or solo travel status, UAE immigration occasionally requests supplementary proof—such as confirmed return tickets, hotel vouchers, host Emirates ID, or bank statements—before releasing the e-Visa.

7) Peak Season Travel Volume

Between November and January (winter holidays, Dubai Shopping Festival, and global expos), daily application volumes surge significantly, extending routine 24-hour turnarounds toward 72+ hours.

8) Agency Delay Before Actual Filing

Sometimes the delay is not with UAE immigration at all—an intermediary or travel agent may batch submissions slowly or wait to verify payment before lodging your application on GDRFA/ICP. Always request your official application reference number to confirm the exact submission timestamp.

3. Diagnostic Guide: What to Do Right Now

Status / Symptom Likely Cause Recommended Action
"In Process" for 4–7 working days Manual security or name-match check Wait for officer clearance; do not file a duplicate application
"Returned / Modification Required" Blurry MRZ scan, photo background, or typo Send high-DPI color scan & white-background photo immediately
"Pending / File Already Exists" Active or uncancelled old UAE visa Verify & cancel previous permit via ICP / GDRFA
No reference number after 24+ hrs Agent has not submitted file yet Request official GDRFA/ICP application number or file with a direct desk

4. Three Mistakes That Make Delays Worse

  • Filing a Second Application Elsewhere: Duplicate submissions while one file is open almost always trigger automatic rejection and forfeit non-refundable government fees.
  • Booking Non-Refundable Flights Before Approval: Always apply 10–14 days ahead or book flexible tickets if your name commonly triggers manual checks.
  • Ignoring Modification Requests: Unanswered re-upload requests auto-cancel after the immigration deadline window.
