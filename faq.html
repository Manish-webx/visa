Clear answers to common questions about Dubai tourist visas, requirements, extensions, and process guidelines.

Standard requirements include a clear color scan of your valid passport (minimum 6 months validity from date of travel), a recent passport-size photograph with white background, and return flight details. Specific nationalities or categories might require proof of accommodation or bank balance statements.

Your passport must have at least 6 months of remaining validity starting from the day you plan to land in the UAE. Passports expiring in less than 6 months will not be approved for tourist visa issuance.

Under normal circumstances, processing takes approximately 24 to 72 business hours once clear and verified documents are submitted. However, processing timelines depend entirely on immigration system security checks and immigration workload.

Yes, tourist and visit visas for Dubai are electronic visas (e-Visas). You do not need to physically visit an embassy or submit physical passport copies. The electronic visa copy is sent via email and WhatsApp upon approval.

Yes, visitors currently inside the UAE on a 30 or 60-day tourist visa can apply for an in-country extension or an airport-to-airport extension depending on current immigration regulations. You must apply before your current visa and grace period expire to avoid overstay fines.

Yes. Multiple-entry visas are available for 30-day, 60-day, and 5-year durations. These are suitable for business travellers, cruise passengers, and frequent visitors who need to enter and exit the UAE several times within the visa validity period.

No. No legitimate service agency can guarantee visa approval. Visa approval or rejection is solely determined by the General Directorate of Residency and Foreigners Affairs (GDRFA) and the Federal Authority for Identity, Citizenship, Customs and Port Security (ICP). Our service provides professional document guidance and pre-submission error checking.

Yes, all travelers, including infants and minors, must hold an individual valid passport and their own visa. When applying for children, a copy of the birth certificate and parents' passport copies are required.

Overstaying your permitted visa duration in the UAE incurs standard fines calculated per day by immigration authorities. We strongly recommend extending your visa or exiting the country before your visa validity period ends.
