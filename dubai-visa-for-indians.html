Dubai Visa for Indians: Complete Guide

Author: Dubai Visa Assistance Team • Published: Jan 10, 2026 • Last Updated: Jan 15, 2026
  1. Home
  2. Blog
  3. Dubai Visa for Indians
Immigration Advisory: Visa requirements and immigration rules can change. Verify current requirements with relevant UAE authorities before travelling.

Dubai remains one of the top international travel destinations for Indian citizens. Whether you are traveling for a quick holiday, shopping festival, destination wedding, or visiting family working in the Emirates, obtaining a Dubai tourist visa is a streamlined electronic process.

Popular Dubai Visa Types for Indian Travellers

Indian passport holders generally choose between two primary single-entry tourist visa options:

30 Days Tourist Visa

Best suited for short vacations (4 to 10 days), honeymoon trips, or attending a trade expo.

60 Days Tourist Visa

Recommended for parents visiting NRI children, extended holidays, and exploratory business visits.

Document Checklist for Indian Applicants

Ensure you have the following documents ready in clear color digital format:

  • Indian Passport: Front page (photo & details) and back page (address page) scanned in high resolution with minimum 6 months validity from travel date.
  • Passport-Size Photograph: Colored photo against a crisp white background (no caps, dark sunglasses, or uniforms).
  • Confirmed Return Flight Ticket: Flight reservation showing departure from India and return from UAE.
  • For Minors: Birth Certificate and copies of parents' valid visas/passports.

What About Visa on Arrival for Indians?

Indian passport holders who hold a valid US Visit Visa (B1/B2), US Green Card, UK Residence Visa, or EU Residence Visa (with at least 6 months validity) are generally eligible for a 14-day visa on arrival at UAE airports. However, travelers without these specific qualifications must obtain a pre-arranged electronic tourist visa prior to departure from India.
