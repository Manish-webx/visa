Dubai Visa Requirements & Checklist

Ensure your travel documents meet UAE immigration standards before applying for a tourist or visit visa.

  1. Home
  2. Visa Requirements
Important Advisory on Visa Regulations

Requirements may vary depending on nationality, visa type, and individual circumstances. UAE immigration authorities (GDRFA / ICP) may update rules periodically. Always verify the current document list with our coordinators before submitting your application.

Mandatory Documents

Standard Document Checklist

These core documents are required for standard 30-day and 60-day Dubai tourist visa applications.

1. Passport Copy

Clear, color scanned copy of the passport biographical page (first & last pages where applicable).

  • Minimum 6 months validity from travel date
  • No cropped edges or glare on photo/text
  • Machine-readable zone (MRZ) must be clear

2. Passport Photograph

Recent colored passport-size photograph conforming to immigration standards.

  • Plain white background
  • Full frontal face view (80% face coverage)
  • No sunglasses or decorative headwear

3. Flight Details

Proof of onward or return travel itinerary indicating expected arrival and departure dates.

  • Return flight reservation
  • Valid for entry at any UAE airport
  • Confirms stay within visa duration
Special Categories

Additional Supporting Documents by Visa Type

Certain special visa categories require supplementary verification documents.

For Minors & Children (Under 18)

When children travel with parents or relatives, the following additional records are required:

  • Birth Certificate (English translation if applicable)
  • Parents' valid passport copies & visas
  • No Objection Certificate (NOC) if traveling with one parent or guardian

For GCC Residence Permit Holders

Residents of Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Qatar, Oman, and Bahrain applying under the GCC track:

  • GCC Residence Permit copy (minimum 3 months validity remaining)
  • Profession on GCC permit must be in the approved category list
  • GCC Iqama copy / Digital Iqama QR code

For UAE 5-Year Multiple Entry Visa

Long-term self-sponsored visa requires financial solvency proof:

  • Bank statement for the last 6 months with a minimum balance equivalent to $4,000 USD (or equivalent currency)
  • Valid UAE health insurance coverage
  • Proof of accommodation or stay arrangements

For Visa Extension Inside UAE

For visitors currently within Dubai or UAE wishing to extend their legal stay:

  • Copy of current valid UAE tourist visa / file number
  • Copy of UAE entry stamp from passport
  • Application must be filed before the existing grace period expires
