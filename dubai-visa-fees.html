Quick Answer & Key Takeaways

Dubai visa total fees depend on four factors: visa duration (48h/96h transit, 14, 30, 60 days, or 5-year), entry type (single vs multiple entry), processing speed (Regular 24–72h vs Express 4–12h), and in-country vs outside-country filing (extensions carry extra UAE inside-country status-change fees). Always confirm all-inclusive pricing before submission.

See what makes up your Dubai visa cost—government immigration fees, mandatory insurance/taxes, processing speed tiers, and how to avoid hidden charges.

2. Why Pre-Checking Saves You Money

Official UAE immigration fees are non-refundable if an application fails due to blurred passport scans, wrong photo backgrounds, or an active uncancelled visa. Using a pre-verified commercial assistance service ensures zero technical errors before government fee payment.
